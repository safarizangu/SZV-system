Our Commitment

Tourism That
Gives Back.

At Safari Zangu Ventures, we believe the privilege of witnessing Tanzania's wildlife comes with a responsibility to protect it. A portion of every booking is directed into the communities and ecosystems that make these experiences possible.

We operate exclusively in private vehicles — which means fewer vehicles in sensitive wildlife zones, less disturbance to animal behaviour, and a more genuine experience for you.

How We Give Back

Our Three Pillars

Community Education

We contribute to school supplies and building maintenance in villages surrounding the Serengeti. Educated communities become the most effective conservation partners.

Women's Cooperatives

We source crafts, produce, and hospitality services from women-led cooperatives in Arusha and Karatu. Your booking creates direct income for these entrepreneurs.

Anti-Poaching Support

A portion of high-end bookings goes directly to ranger training and equipment for anti-poaching patrols in the Serengeti ecosystem. Wildlife tourism only works if wildlife exists.
